\section{Evidence provenance}
\label{app:provenance}

% CLAIM: C13,C14,C16,C17
The manuscript is generated from a machine-readable evidence registry rather
than from values copied into prose.  Each registered source has a stable
identifier, repository-relative path, commit and SHA-256 identity, temporal
class, test-visibility state, causal and deployment boundary, exact
denominator, permitted use, forbidden interpretation, status, and publication
permissions.  The claim registry maps stable claim identifiers to those
sources and permitted manuscript locations.  A content-addressed logic lock
binds the charter, argument outline, notation contract, scope, claims,
evidence registry, evidence audit, and adversarial logic review.

statistical estimate completed, after which the result froze as

The report-only aid preserves all
\VFourSameInformationFailureCount/\num{\VFourSameInformationAuditCycleCount}{}

Empirical macros in \texttt{generated/numbers.tex}, table fragments, and figure
PDFs are produced by repository scripts.  Per-number provenance records the
evidence ID, source path and digest, row/field selector or calculation, raw and
formatted value, rounding rule, and generator identity.  Generated tables and
the V4 figure carry separate provenance manifests.  Static checks reject
hand-copied protected values, unknown claim annotations, unresolved draft
markers, forbidden wording, missing citations, and nonportable local paths.

Frozen V3 evidence is checked against its original SHA-256 inventory.  Frozen
V4 protocol objects, the bounded root index, every indexed artifact, the
terminal protocol state, and the report-only aids are checked independently.
Both trees are read-only inputs to the paper pipeline.  These controls
establish traceability and repeatability of the reported calculations; they do
not repair confounding, override a failed gate, create hardware evidence, or
promote the single development trace to independent confirmation.
