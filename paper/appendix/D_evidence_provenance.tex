\section{Evidence provenance}
\label{app:provenance}

% CLAIM: C13
The manuscript is generated from a machine-readable evidence registry rather
than from values copied into prose.  Each registered source has an evidence
identifier, repository-relative path, temporal class (current, frozen, or
post-freeze), permitted and forbidden uses, and a SHA-256 digest.  The claim
registry maps stable identifiers C01--C13, N01--N03, and E01 to those sources
and to permitted manuscript locations.  A logic-lock file records the hashes
of the charter, argument outline, notation contract, scope, claim matrix,
evidence audit, adversarial logic review, and the immutable v3 hash inventory.

Empirical macros in \texttt{generated/numbers.tex}, table fragments, and figure
PDFs are produced by repository scripts.  A number-provenance manifest records
the source file, selector, field or calculation, raw value, formatted value,
and generated macro for every protected result.  Static checks reject
hand-copied protected values, unknown claim annotations, uncited or unknown
bibliography keys, unresolved draft markers, and forbidden claim phrases.
Generated displays carry a generation manifest so that source changes are
detectable even when the rendered output remains visually similar.

Frozen v3 evidence is additionally checked against the repository's recorded
SHA-256 inventory before and after paper generation.  The source artifacts are
read-only inputs: the paper scripts recompute bounded summaries and
visualizations without rewriting the frozen bytes.  The current profile-aware
implementation and compatibility regression are labelled post-freeze and
therefore cannot silently replace or reinterpret the frozen experimental
population.

This machinery establishes traceability and repeatability of the reported
calculations.  It does not repair confounding, create missing derivative truth,
or promote a development observation to independent confirmation.
